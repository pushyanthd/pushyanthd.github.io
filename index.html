02 / About

The work behind
the models.

My projects explore what makes AI systems useful beyond a model response. That means tracing outputs to evidence, measuring quality before changing a pipeline, and giving people control over consequential actions.

Across this portfolio, the recurring questions are practical: Can an output be checked? Does a change improve the measured result? Can an interrupted workflow recover?
